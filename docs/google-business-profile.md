## 6. Reviews

1. In the profile, choose **Ask for reviews** and copy the short link (it looks like `https://g.page/r/…/review`).
2. Send it on WhatsApp to every happy guest after the ride, for example:
   *"Thank you for riding with Avish Taxi! If you have a minute, a Google review helps a lot: <link>"*
3. Reply to every review, politely and quickly.
4. Never buy reviews or post reviews yourself. Google removes them and can suspend the profile.

## 7. Send these back to update the website

Once the profile is live, send:
- the **review link** (`https://g.page/r/…/review`)
- the **profile / Maps link** (Share → Copy link, for example `https://maps.app.goo.gl/…`)
- any **Facebook / Instagram** links

These will be added to the website's "Read reviews" and "Leave a review" buttons, the footer, and the structured data (`sameAs` and `hasMap`). That connects the website and the Google profile as one business.
