Good to know

Frequently asked questions

How do I book a taxi in Tangalle?

The fastest way is WhatsApp. Send your pickup, destination, date, time and number of passengers, and you'll get a fixed price and confirmation. You can also use the booking form below or call directly.

Will you meet me at Colombo Airport?

Yes. Share your flight number when you book and your driver will be waiting at arrivals with a name board. If your flight is delayed, we adjust the pickup time. No stress.

Are prices per person or per vehicle?

Per vehicle. The price you agree on WhatsApp covers the whole car or van for your group, with no hidden fees added later.

How long is the drive from Colombo Airport to Tangalle?

Usually around 3 hours using the Southern Expressway, depending on traffic and the time of day.

Can you arrange a Yala safari from Tangalle?

Yes. We can pick you up early from your hotel in Tangalle, drive you to Yala and arrange the safari jeep, then bring you back the same day.

Do you have child seats?

Child seats are available on request. Please mention the children's ages when you book so we can prepare.
