        Good to know

        Frequently asked questions

        How do I book a taxi in Tangalle?

        The fastest way is WhatsApp. Send your pickup, destination, date, time and number of passengers, and you'll get a fixed price and confirmation. You can also use the booking form below or call directly.

        Will you meet me at Colombo Airport?

        Yes. Share your flight number when you book and your driver will be waiting at arrivals with a name board. If your flight is delayed, we adjust the pickup time. No stress.

        Are prices per person or per vehicle?

        Per vehicle. The price you agree on WhatsApp covers the whole car, van or shuttle for your group, with no hidden fees added later.

        How long is the drive from Colombo Airport to Tangalle?

        Usually around 3 hours using the Southern Expressway, depending on traffic and the time of day.

        Can you arrange a Yala safari from Tangalle?

        Yes. We can pick you up early from your hotel in Tangalle, drive you to Yala and arrange the safari jeep, then bring you back the same day.

        Do you have child seats?

        Child seats are available on request. Please mention the children's ages when you book so we can prepare.

        How much is a taxi from Colombo Airport to Tangalle?

        A private car from Colombo Airport (CMB) to Tangalle is LKR 25,000 and a van or shuttle is LKR 33,000. The price is per vehicle, not per person. Prices can be negotiated and may change from day to day, but we always give you an affordable rate, confirmed on WhatsApp before you travel.

        How much is a taxi from Tangalle to Ella?

        Tangalle to Ella takes about 3 hours and is LKR 23,000 for a car and LKR 27,000 for a van or shuttle. Stops at waterfalls or viewpoints on the way can be added on request.

        Which currency are your prices in?

        All prices are quoted in Sri Lankan Rupees (LKR) for the whole vehicle. They can be negotiated and may change from day to day, but we always give you an affordable rate. Ask on WhatsApp if you have a question about payment.
