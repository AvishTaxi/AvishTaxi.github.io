Sri Lanka Taxi Guide for Tourists

Quick answer

The easiest way for foreign visitors to travel in Sri Lanka is a private taxi with an English-speaking driver, booked on WhatsApp and priced per vehicle. From Tangalle on the south coast, trips start at LKR 9,000 (Mirissa) and Colombo Airport is LKR 25,000 by car. Prices can be negotiated and may change from day to day, but we will always give you an affordable rate.

How to get around Sri Lanka as a tourist

  • Private taxi with driver: the most comfortable choice for airport transfers, day trips and multi-day tours. You travel door to door in an air-conditioned vehicle and stop wherever you like.
  • Tuk-tuk: great for short hops around town and to nearby beaches. Agree the fare before you get in.
  • Train: famous for the scenic hill-country line around Ella and Kandy, but seats can sell out and stations are not always near your hotel.
  • Bus: very cheap, but crowded and slow with luggage.
  • Ride-hailing apps: useful in Colombo and larger cities, with fewer cars in smaller coastal towns.

Tips for booking a taxi in Sri Lanka

  • Agree the price first. Confirm the total price per vehicle on WhatsApp before you travel, so there are no surprises.
  • Share your flight number for airport pickups, so the driver can follow your landing time.
  • Tell the driver about luggage, surfboards and children so the right vehicle and child seats are ready.
  • Plan for realistic driving times. Roads are winding outside the expressways; allow extra time for stops.
  • Start early for safaris and whale watching. Yala and Udawalawe game drives and Mirissa boats start around dawn.
  • Keep your driver's WhatsApp number. It is the fastest way to change plans while you travel.

Arriving at Colombo Airport (CMB)

After immigration and baggage claim, walk out to the arrivals hall where your driver waits with a name board. The drive to Tangalle on the expressway takes about 3 to 3½ hours. See the Colombo Airport to Tangalle taxi page for prices and booking.

Questions

Sri Lanka taxi FAQ for visitors

What is the best way for tourists to get around Sri Lanka?

For most visitors a private car with a driver is the most comfortable and flexible option: door-to-door pickups, air conditioning, room for luggage and stops wherever you like. Trains are scenic for the hill country, tuk-tuks are good for short local trips, and ride-hailing apps work mainly in larger cities.

How much does a taxi cost in Sri Lanka?

Private taxi prices are usually quoted per vehicle for the whole trip. From Tangalle, for example, Mirissa is LKR 9,000 by car and Colombo Airport is LKR 25,000 by car. Prices can be negotiated and may change from day to day, but we will always give you an affordable rate.

Should I pay per person or per vehicle?

Private taxis in Sri Lanka are normally priced per vehicle, so the price is shared by your whole group. Always agree the price before you travel.

Which currency should I use to pay for a taxi?

Prices are quoted in Sri Lankan Rupees (LKR). Ask your driver on WhatsApp before the trip if you have a question about payment.

How do I get from Colombo Airport to the south coast?

The easiest way is a pre-booked private taxi. The drive from Colombo Airport (CMB) to Tangalle takes about 3 to 3½ hours on the expressway and costs LKR 25,000 by car. Your driver meets you at arrivals with a name board.

When is the best time to visit the south coast?

The south and west coasts are usually driest from about November to April, which is also the main whale-watching season off Mirissa.
