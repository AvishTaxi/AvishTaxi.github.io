Tangalle to Yala taxi and Yala safari from Tangalle

Go from Tangalle to Yala National Park and Tissamaharama by private taxi, either as a transfer to your safari lodge or as a Yala safari day trip. We can pick you up early so you reach the park gate for the morning game drive, when leopard sightings are best.

All prices are in Sri Lankan Rupees (LKR) for the whole vehicle, not per person, with no hidden fees. Prices can be negotiated and may change from day to day, but we will always give you an affordable rate. Message us on WhatsApp for today's best price.

What's included

  • A friendly, English-speaking local driver from Tangalle
  • A clean, air-conditioned car, van or shuttle with seat belts
  • Help with luggage and pickup from your hotel, villa or arrivals hall
  • Stops for photos, food or toilets when you ask
  • Child seats on request (mention the children's ages when you book)

The route

The drive is about 80 km to Tissamaharama and usually takes about 1¾ hours via the A2 through Hambantota. Popular stops on the way include Hambantota, Bundala bird sanctuary or Tissa Wewa lake.

How to book

  1. Tap Book on WhatsApp and fill in the short form (date, time, passengers, pickup point).
  2. Send the ready-made message to Avishka on WhatsApp +94 76 562 7939.
  3. Get your confirmed price, usually within minutes, and relax: your driver will be there on time.

Questions

Tangalle to Yala / Tissamaharama taxi: FAQ

How much is a taxi from Tangalle to Yala / Tissamaharama?

A private car from Tangalle to Yala / Tissamaharama costs LKR 20,000 (1–3 passengers) and a van or shuttle costs LKR 23,000 (4–8 passengers). The price is per vehicle, not per person. Prices can be negotiated and may change from day to day, but we will always give you an affordable rate.

How long is the drive from Tangalle to Yala / Tissamaharama?

It is about 80 km to Tissamaharama and usually takes about 1¾ hours via the A2 through Hambantota, depending on traffic and stops.

Can we stop on the way?

Yes. Popular stops include Hambantota, Bundala bird sanctuary or Tissa Wewa lake. Tell us when you book and we will plan the time.

How do I book?

Tap Book on WhatsApp, fill in the short form and send the message to Avishka on +94 76 562 7939. You get a confirmed price, usually within minutes. Booking is available 24/7.

Can you arrange the Yala safari jeep?

Yes. We can drive you to Yala, arrange the safari jeep and tickets, and bring you back to Tangalle the same day. Ask on WhatsApp for a full day-trip price.

What time should we leave Tangalle for a morning safari?

Early, usually before dawn, so you are at the park gate when it opens. We confirm the exact pickup time when you book.
